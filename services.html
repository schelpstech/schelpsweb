How we approach a project

We consider the people, processes and operational requirements alongside the technology.

  1. Understand your institution

    Discuss the problem, users, existing systems and priorities.

  2. Define the scope

    Agree the deliverables, configuration, integrations and support requirements.

  3. Develop and deploy

    Build or configure the solution and prepare the institution for use.

  4. Support adoption

    Discuss training, ongoing support and improvements as part of the engagement.
