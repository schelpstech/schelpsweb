From teaching to reporting

An assessment should
fit the academic workflow.

LearnAble connects CBT with school records, while giving teachers and academic administrators control over marking, publication and score transfer.

Assessments can support weekly scores, continuous assessment, examinations or practice, according to how the school configures them.

  1. Prepare the paper

    Create questions for allocated subjects and scheme topics, then set the assessment schedule.

  2. Let learners take the assessment

    Eligible learners access timed attempts with answer saving and supported reconnection.

  3. Mark and review

    Objective answers are marked automatically; teachers review and mark written responses.

  4. Approve, publish and transfer

    Academic administrators approve completed results before publication. Authorised transfers map relevant scores into school reports.

Before we get started

A few practical questions.

Can LearnAble be set up around our school?

The platform supports school profile information, academic terms, classes, subjects, fee definitions and result settings. SCHELPS can discuss your workflows and the configuration needed for your deployment.

Does the finance module include online payment processing?

The platform described here manages fee assignments, discounts, payment records, receipts and reports. Online payment-provider integration is a separate requirement to discuss during implementation.

Does LearnAble have a parent portal?

The current platform provides learner, instructor, administrator and bursary access. A dedicated parent or guardian account module would need to be scoped separately.

Can CBT results contribute to school reports?

Yes. Marked and published assessment results can be transferred by an authorised user to weekly, CA or examination scores, using the school's configured score limits. Practice assessments do not contribute to official results.

How do we see the platform and discuss pricing?

Contact SCHELPS to arrange a demonstration and discuss school size, required modules, setup, training and support. A proposal can then be prepared for your school's requirements.
