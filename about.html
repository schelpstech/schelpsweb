Who we are

Built from experience
inside education.

Our team brings together software developers, systems architects and organisational process modellers, with over 20 years of combined professional experience.

We work with educational institutions, examination bodies, training organisations and public-sector stakeholders. Our work spans school portals, learning and assessment platforms, academic reporting, campus networking and education data services.

We approach each project by understanding the institution's processes, then shaping the technology around the people who will use it.
